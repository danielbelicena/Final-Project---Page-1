WELCOME TO YOUR GAMING HUB

This presentation introduces action, adventure, and survival games that improve strategy, reflexes, and critical thinking.

Featured Game: Uncharted 4

Uncharted 4 is a cinematic action-adventure game developed by Naughty Dog.

You play as Nathan Drake exploring lost cities, solving puzzles, and surviving intense combat situations.

Why Play Uncharted 4?

Improves decision making

Enhances problem-solving skills

Develops hand-eye coordination

Featured Game: Resident Evil 4

Resident Evil 4 is a survival horror game where players must manage resources and survive dangerous enemies.

It builds strategic thinking and awareness under pressure.

Why Play Resident Evil 4?

Improves reaction time

Enhances strategic planning

Builds focus and patience

MASTER YOUR GAMING SKILLS

Gaming Movement & Aim

Practice in training modes to improve movement, accuracy, and reaction speed.

Movement Basics

Use WASD (PC) or left analog stick (Console) to move.

Use mouse or right analog stick to aim and look around.

Master strafing, sprinting, and positioning.

Tips to Become a Better Gamer

Practice daily

Adjust sensitivity settings

Learn from mistakes

Take breaks to avoid burnout
